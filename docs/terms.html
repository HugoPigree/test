Terms of Service

What Autoclip does

Autoclip watches public Twitch chats, cuts short clips from public live streams, adds subtitles, a title and background music, and uploads selected clips to the TikTok account that authorized it through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the connected TikTok account uses Autoclip. Nobody else can sign in, upload or post through it.

Content

  • Every clip credits the streamer and links to the original Twitch stream in its caption.
  • Clips flagged as possibly breaking TikTok's Community Guidelines (nudity, hate, real violence) are never posted.
  • The account owner is responsible for what is published on their account and follows TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • A streamer who wants a clip removed can ask the account owner through a comment or a direct message on the TikTok account, and the clip will be deleted.

Limits

Autoclip posts at most five videos a day, at least one hour apart. It is provided as is, without any warranty, and may stop at any time.
